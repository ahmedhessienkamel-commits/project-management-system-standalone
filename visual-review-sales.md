# Visual review — Sales and collections redesign

- `/sales` renders as a dedicated Arabic RTL command center with a dark/gold hero header, sales/collected/outstanding metrics, and tabs for overview, invoice creation, collections and escrow, customers, and unit inventory.
- The invoice workflow is presented as the primary action and includes customer, project, unit, stage, item description, tax, related document, status, and automatic total sections.
- `/accounting` no longer displays a sales-invoice card; the visible accounting document cards are purchase invoice, journal entry, payment voucher, receipt voucher, quotation, and purchase order.
- The current screenshots show no layout overflow in the desktop viewport. The invoice and collection tabs should still receive a focused interaction test before release.
